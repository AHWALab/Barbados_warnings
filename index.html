Uncertainty quantified flood potential for Barbados, from the TITO EF5 CREST ensemble on the 30 m island domain. Flood potential is classed on maximum unit streamflow in m3/s per km2, read at the ensemble 90th percentile and clustered into zones. Both cases use the same FLASH class bands, 0.5, 1, 2 and 4, so they can be read against each other and against the Guatemala product.
